Files starting with `_` are ignored by the content loader, so this template is
never built and never published. Copy it, drop the underscore, and edit.

The page title is rendered from `title` as the `<h1>`, so start the body at
`##` — do not repeat the project name as a heading. Status, stack and the
repository link all come from the frontmatter and render above the content;
writing them into the prose as well would mean maintaining them twice.

## What it does

Lead with the thing itself, in plain terms, before any architecture.

## The interesting decision

The part worth reading. What the trade-off was, what you chose, and what it
cost. A decision with a reason you can reconstruct is worth more than a list of
technologies.

## What isn't done

Being specific about the gaps is more credible than implying there are none.
